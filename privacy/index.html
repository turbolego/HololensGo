Privacy Policy

HololensGo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The following platform capabilities are used exclusively for the app's on-device functionality:

CapabilityUsage
webcam Used for HoloLens spatial mapping and head tracking so the app can understand the room layout and place Mickey Mouse in your environment. No images are recorded or transmitted.
microphone Used for voice commands. No audio is recorded or transmitted.
internetClient Not currently used. Reserved for future leaderboard features.

No data leaves your HoloLens. This app is a single-player game with no network functionality.
